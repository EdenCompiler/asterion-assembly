# Store copy / Texto de loja

## English

**Build exact. Expand carefully. End the hive.**

Asterion Assembly is an open-source factory automation game set on a dark,
bioluminescent world. Route dense belt networks, process fluids, balance power,
signal trains, deploy logistics drones and defend a persistent colony through
nine escalating chapters. Finish the campaign on your terms, then keep the same
world as an unlimited sandbox. Includes full keyboard/mouse and gamepad control,
English and Portuguese, deterministic seeds, four difficulty presets and mods.

## Português

**Construa com precisão. Expanda com cuidado. Destrua a colmeia.**

Asterion Assembly é um jogo open source de automação industrial em um mundo
escuro e bioluminescente. Organize esteiras, processe fluidos, equilibre energia,
sinalize ferrovias, use drones e defenda uma colônia persistente em nove
capítulos. Após a campanha, continue no mesmo mundo em sandbox.

Suggested tags: Automation, Base Building, Strategy, Trains, Open Source,
